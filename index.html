Settings

Pharmacies (one per line, format: name | google-maps-url, prefix with * for your pharmacy)
Open the pharmacy on Google Maps, copy the URL from the address bar, paste it here. The form above uses this list.
Bulk paste (one line per pharmacy: name | rating | reviewCount | s1,s2,s3,s4,s5)
Alternative to the per-row form if you'd rather paste everything at once.
Storage
Everything stays on your machine — pharmacy list and snapshots live in localStorage. No server, no account, no API key. Auto-scraping Google Maps from a local page isn't a viable option (their HTML doesn't include rating data, and proxy services that fetched it are either rate-limited or have moved to paid plans) — but reading off four numbers a week and typing them in takes less than a minute, and the dashboard does the analytics.
